## Summary

I wanted Security+ done properly but fast, so I gave it about a week of focused, daily study. Instead of watching videos front to back, I went practice-questions first: take a quiz, get things wrong, figure out why, write it down. I used ExamCompass for topic-by-topic quizzes, Dion Training's practice exams for the real-exam feel, and CyberCraft's free YouTube course to fill gaps.

Every note went into Obsidian through my [Claudsidian](https://github.com/itsvir10/claudsidian) setup, and by the end I had a full guide organised by the five exam domains, plus a one-line-each glossary of every acronym on the exam. I passed SY0-701, cleaned the notes up, and put them on GitHub so other people can use them too.

## What I learned

- Getting questions wrong teaches you faster than watching someone explain things. Practice tests first, videos second.
- Writing notes in my own words is what made things stick. Copying definitions never did.
- The acronyms are half the battle. A plain one-line glossary saved me a lot of second-guessing.
- Cross-checking against the official exam objectives kept me from going down rabbit holes on stuff that isn't tested.
